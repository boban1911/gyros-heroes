# Initial Concept\n\n
\n\n## Product Definition

### Project Goal
To build a high-fidelity, single-page application for \"Gyros Heroes,\" a fast-food restaurant, that perfectly matches the provided Figma design specifications. The site will serve as a digital storefront for local customers and potential employees.

### Target Audience
- **Primary:** Local customers seeking menu information, locations, and quick ordering options.
- **Secondary:** Potential employees interested in joining the team.

### Key Features & Sections
- **Hero Section:** \"Hero is in Town\" with city/location-specific Order & Pickup call-to-actions.
- **About Us:** Brand story (\"O Nama\") section.
- **Menu Showcase:** detailed visual menu with categories (Classic Hero, Veliki Hero, Kids Hero, etc.), item descriptions, and images.
- **Locations & Gallery:** Visual gallery of store locations and interior atmosphere.
- **Recruitment:** \"Pridruži se Hero timu!\" section with a link to apply.
- **Testimonials:** \"Šta naši gosti kažu\" section featuring customer reviews.
- **Footer:** Quick links, social media icons, and copyright information.

### User Interactions
- **Ordering:** \"Poruči\" buttons will link directly to external delivery platforms (Glovo, Wolt) or location-specific pickup pages.
- **Navigation:** Smooth scrolling to anchor sections on the single page.

### Localization
- **Language:** Serbian (exclusive).

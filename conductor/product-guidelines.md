# Product Guidelines

## Visual Identity
- **Aesthetic:** High-Energy & Bold.
- **Palette:** Deep Navy Blue (#1B2A4E), Vibrant Yellow (#FFC107), and accent colors from the "Hero" brand.
- **Graphic Elements:** Strong outlines, dynamic container shapes, and prominent "Hero" themed icons.
- **Images:** High-quality food photography and atmospheric restaurant interior shots.

## Tone of Voice
- **Style:** Informal & Enthusiastic.
- **Language:** Serbian.
- **Messaging:** Friendly, energetic, and community-focused (e.g., "Pridruži se Hero timu!"). Use food puns and hero-related metaphors where appropriate.

## Typography & Spacing
- **Implementation:** Design-Driven Tokens.
- **Standards:** Strictly adhere to Figma's font families, weights, and spacing.
- **Responsiveness:** Maintain 1:1 visual parity on desktop while implementing a fluid, responsive scale for mobile and tablet views to preserve hierarchy.

## User Experience (UX)
- **Navigation:** Single-page smooth scroll.
- **Clarity:** Visual hierarchy must clearly distinguish menu categories and location details.
- **Trust:** Display authentic customer testimonials prominently.
